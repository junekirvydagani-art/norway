01 / THE MUSEUM

History is more
than a date.

History can be held in the grain of a carved wooden ship, traced along a church portal, or stitched into a ceremonial garment. Here, the past is experienced through the objects and traditions that carry it.

Explore a curated visual introduction to Norwegian heritage, from the Viking Age to the living cultures of today. Select any artifact to enter its digital gallery label and discover its historical context.

THE LIVING MUSEUM

A culture
still in motion.

Norwegian culture is not confined to display cases. Traditional arts, Sámi cultural life, folk music and the knowledge of making and repairing remain part of the present. Explore what objects can tell us, and what they cannot tell us alone.
